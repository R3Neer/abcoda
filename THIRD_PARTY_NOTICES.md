## FluidR3 General MIDI samples

At playback time, abcjs loads only the required instrument/note samples from its default FluidR3_GM mirror at `https://paulrosen.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/`.

The upstream [midi-js-soundfonts project](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) identifies FluidR3_GM as Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. ABCoda includes an in-widget attribution link. Before public marketplace submission, have the final attribution wording reviewed for the intended distribution territories.

No Musyng Kite samples are selected by ABCoda because that bank uses CC BY-SA 3.0 and would add unnecessary share-alike considerations.
